Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VELTRIX (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use the VELTRIX Discord bot and website.

Before publishing, ensure this policy accurately reflects the data your deployed service actually collects, stores, and deletes.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled, VELTRIX may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, server settings, command activity, moderation reports, incident records, audit logs, technical error logs, and information submitted through support requests. If an ER:LC integration is enabled, VELTRIX may also process information made available through authorized API access.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate bot commands and website features, save server configurations, provide moderation and security tools, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and maintain service reliability.

3. ER:LC Credentials

ER:LC server keys and other integration credentials are sensitive. They should be stored securely, never exposed in public frontend code or logs, and used only for authorized features. Do not submit credentials through public support channels.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers, shared with Discord or ER:LC as needed to provide integrations, made available to authorized administrators for their own community records, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to address abuse and security incidents.

VELTRIX is intended to keep each community’s private configuration and records separate. Access should be limited according to authorization and permissions.

5. Storage, Security, and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as needed to operate the service, maintain security records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the feature and the service’s actual implementation.

6. Requests and Deletion

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information, contact us using the support details below. We may verify your identity or authority over a community before processing a request. Some records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons. Removing the bot may not automatically delete stored records.

7. Third-Party Services

VELTRIX may connect to third-party services such as Discord and ER:LC. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices, which apply to their platforms.

8. Children’s Privacy

Users must comply with applicable age requirements and privacy laws when using VELTRIX.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide notice of significant changes.
