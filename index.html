Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinel, a Discord security and moderation bot, may process information when it is used in a Discord server.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, Sentinel may process Discord user IDs and usernames, server/channel/role and configuration IDs, moderation and security logs, reports and appeals, relevant message content used for moderation or security features, and evidence submitted by users or administrators.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate Sentinel, apply configured server rules, detect suspicious activity, maintain security and moderation logs, process reports and appeals, troubleshoot issues, and maintain reliability.

3. Honeypot Security

If a server enables the honeypot feature, Sentinel may record information related to a trigger, such as the user ID, username, server, message content, timestamp, and moderation action. A honeypot trigger is a security signal and is not absolute proof that an account has been compromised.

4. Data Sharing

Sentinel does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers needed to operate the Bot, or disclosed when reasonably necessary for legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for security, moderation, appeals, troubleshooting, and operational purposes.

6. Discord

Sentinel operates through Discord and may process information made available through the Discord platform. Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own policies.

7. Server Administrator Responsibility

Server administrators are responsible for configuring Sentinel appropriately and providing their members with any notices required for their use of moderation and security features.

8. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information handled by Sentinel, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Last Updated date will be changed when material updates are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Sentinel development team through the official Sentinel support server or support channel.
